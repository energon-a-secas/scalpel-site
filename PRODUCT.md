# Scalpel: product one-pager

**What it is:** Dissect a Claude skill from a zip or pasted SKILL.md: router quality, body structure, scripts and references, and the caveats to fix before it ships

**Who it is for:** Someone authoring or reviewing Claude skills: they just wrote a
SKILL.md, or one landed in a PR, and they want the caveats named before it gets
installed or merged.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** At the desk mid-review: a skill folder arrives in a PR or a chat,
the reviewer zips it, drops it on the page, and reads the caveat report before
deciding whether it ships.

**What done looks like:** A report the author can act on without arguing: every
blocker names its rule and its line, and three A grades mean the skill is ready
for a human read, not that it skipped one.
